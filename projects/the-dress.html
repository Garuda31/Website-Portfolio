MSc · MEGA · 2024

The Dress

MEGA is a multidisciplinary studio project in which a team of six students, each specialized in a different discipline, designed the transformation of Hoog Catharijne in Utrecht into a mixed-use complex combining an urban mining lab, a distribution centre, housing, offices and a hotel. Together with Valerie de Horde, I was responsible for the structural design, working closely with the architectural, façade, climate and computational designers to translate their ambitions into a feasible structural system.


My work started with analysing the existing Gildenkwartier and Radboudkwartier structures, calculating the remaining capacity of the existing concrete columns to determine how much additional load they could carry. Based on this, I developed the structural systems for the new additions: a steel truss structure for the urban mining lab that reuses and extends the existing concrete core, and a steel frame with SHS columns, HEB450 beams and prefabricated timber Kerto-Ripa floors for the twenty-one-floor residential highrise placed on top of the Gildenkwartier. Steel and timber were chosen for their efficiency, low weight and compatibility with bolted, dry connections, allowing the structure to be assembled and later disassembled in line with the project’s circular design ambitions.
