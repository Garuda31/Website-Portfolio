De Groene Loper

De Groene Loper was a ten-week bachelor group project in which a team of ten students, each assigned a distinct role, developed a 50-year spatial vision for Den Haag Zuidwest, focusing on the neighbourhoods Moerwijk-West and Morgenstond-Zuid. My role within the team was GIS-expert, responsible for collecting, analysing and visualising spatial data, from real estate and housing figures to infrastructure and regional transport networks, and structuring it so the rest of the team could use it to substantiate their design decisions.


Using QGIS, I mapped and analysed density, housing and real estate values, amenities, public transport accessibility and regional infrastructure to identify where densification made the most sense and how the surrounding road, cycling and public transport network needed to adapt to support it. This spatial analysis directly informed our final proposal, De Groene Loper: a car-free green route running through the neighbourhood that connects a new market square, sports park and community facility while linking Moerwijk station to the wider green structure of the city.


Tools/Approach:

QGIS, Grasshopper, Rhino, Miro, Site visit
